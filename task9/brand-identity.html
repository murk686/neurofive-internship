07 — Brand Guidelines

Usage Rules

These guidelines ensure NexaFlow looks consistent and professional everywhere it appears.

Voice & Tone

  • Confident, not arrogant
  • Technical, but human
  • Concise — every word earns its place
  • Empowering, never condescending

Logo Rules

  • Minimum size: 120px wide
  • Clear space = height of "N"
  • Never stretch or skew the mark
  • Never place on busy photography

Color Usage

  • Midnight is the dominant brand color
  • Flow Blue for interactive elements only
  • Never use more than 3 brand colors at once
  • Ensure WCAG AA contrast at all times

Typography

  • Space Grotesk for all headings
  • Inter for body and UI text
  • Never mix more than 2 type scales
  • Minimum body size: 14px

✓ Do

  • Use approved color combinations
  • Maintain logo clear space
  • Use Space Grotesk for headlines
  • Keep messaging concise
  • Use the icon mark at small sizes

✗ Don't

  • Stretch or rotate the logo
  • Use unapproved colors
  • Place logo on low-contrast bg
  • Use shadows or outlines on logo
  • Mix with competitor branding
